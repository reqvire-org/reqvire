# Elements

### Broken SKOS Ontology

Ontology that intentionally references an unknown SKOS class through SHACL.

#### Metadata
  * type: ontology
  * ontology_base: https://example.test/broken-skos
  * ontology_prefix: broken

#### Ontology
```turtle
@prefix broken: <https://example.test/broken-skos#> .
@prefix owl: <http://www.w3.org/2002/07/owl#> .

<https://example.test/broken-skos> a owl:Ontology .

broken:Thing a owl:Class .
```
---

### Broken SKOS Requirement

The system shall reject SHACL references to SKOS terms that are not present in the built-in SKOS external source.

#### Metadata
  * type: requirement

#### Relations
  * constrainedBy: [Broken SKOS Shape](#broken-skos-shape)
---

### Broken SKOS Shape

This semantic contract intentionally references a missing SKOS class.

#### Metadata
  * type: semantic-contract

#### Relations
  * constrain: [Broken SKOS Requirement](#broken-skos-requirement)
  * use: [Broken SKOS Ontology](#broken-skos-ontology)

#### Shapes
```turtle
@prefix broken: <https://example.test/broken-skos#> .
@prefix sh: <http://www.w3.org/ns/shacl#> .
@prefix skos: <http://www.w3.org/2004/02/skos/core#> .

broken:BrokenShape
  a sh:NodeShape ;
  sh:targetClass skos:NotARealSkosClass .
```
---
