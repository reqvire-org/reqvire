# Elements

### Semantic Capability

Capability root.

#### Metadata
  * type: capability

#### Attachments
  * [Semantic Ontology](#semantic-ontology)
---

### API Requirement

Requirement constrained through direct constrainedBy command.

#### Metadata
  * type: requirement

#### Relations
  * specify: [Semantic Capability](#semantic-capability)
---

### Audit Requirement

Requirement constrained through inverse constrain command.

#### Metadata
  * type: requirement

#### Relations
  * specify: [Semantic Capability](#semantic-capability)
---

### Semantic Ontology

Ontology vocabulary used by linked semantic contracts.

#### Metadata
  * type: ontology
  * ontology_base: https://example.test/link-semantic
  * ontology_prefix: lsc

#### Ontology
```turtle
@prefix lsc: <https://example.test/link-semantic#> .
@prefix owl: <http://www.w3.org/2002/07/owl#> .
@prefix rdfs: <http://www.w3.org/2000/01/rdf-schema#> .

<https://example.test/link-semantic> a owl:Ontology .

lsc:APIRequirement a owl:Class .
lsc:AuditRequirement a owl:Class .
lsc:hasEvidence a owl:ObjectProperty .
```
---

### Extra Semantic Ontology

Additional ontology used to prove CLI `use` and `usedBy` relation writes.

#### Metadata
  * type: ontology
  * ontology_base: https://example.test/link-semantic-extra
  * ontology_prefix: lscx

#### Relations
  * derivedFrom: [Semantic Ontology](#semantic-ontology)

#### Ontology
```turtle
@prefix lscx: <https://example.test/link-semantic-extra#> .
@prefix owl: <http://www.w3.org/2002/07/owl#> .

<https://example.test/link-semantic-extra> a owl:Ontology .

<https://example.test/link-semantic-extra> owl:imports <https://example.test/link-semantic> .

lscx:ExtraContext a owl:Class .
```
---

### API Shape Contract

Shape linked through `use` and direct requirement `constrainedBy`.

#### Metadata
  * type: semantic-contract

#### Relations
  * use: [Semantic Ontology](#semantic-ontology)

#### Shapes
```turtle
@prefix lsc: <https://example.test/link-semantic#> .
@prefix sh: <http://www.w3.org/ns/shacl#> .

lsc:APIRequirementShape
  a sh:NodeShape ;
  sh:targetClass lsc:APIRequirement ;
  sh:property [
    sh:path lsc:hasEvidence ;
    sh:minCount 1 ;
  ] .
```
---
