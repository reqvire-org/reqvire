### Platform Capability

Platform capability.

#### Metadata
  * type: capability

#### Relations
  * specifiedBy: [Billing Platform Requirement](#billing-platform-requirement)
---

### Platform Ontology

Platform ontology.

#### Metadata
  * type: ontology
  * ontology_base: https://example.test/platform
  * ontology_prefix: platform

#### Ontology
```turtle
@prefix platform: <https://example.test/platform#> .
@prefix owl: <http://www.w3.org/2002/07/owl#> .

<https://example.test/platform> a owl:Ontology .
platform:TenantScopedEvent a owl:Class .
platform:tenant a owl:ObjectProperty .
platform:eventSubject a owl:ObjectProperty .
```
---

### Billing Ontology

Billing ontology derived from the platform ontology while declaring its own ontology base.

#### Metadata
  * type: ontology
  * ontology_base: https://example.test/billing
  * ontology_prefix: billing

#### Relations
  * derivedFrom: [Platform Ontology](#platform-ontology)

#### Ontology
```turtle
@prefix billing: <https://example.test/billing#> .
@prefix platform: <https://example.test/platform#> .
@prefix owl: <http://www.w3.org/2002/07/owl#> .

<https://example.test/billing>
  a owl:Ontology ;
  owl:imports <https://example.test/platform> .

billing:InvoiceEvent a owl:Class .
billing:invoice a owl:ObjectProperty .
billing:lineItem a owl:ObjectProperty .
billing:status a owl:DatatypeProperty .
```
---

### Billing Platform Requirement

The system shall validate billing event shapes against billing and inherited platform ontology terms.

#### Metadata
  * type: requirement

#### Relations
  * specify: [Platform Capability](#platform-capability)
  * constrainedBy: [Billing Shape Contract](#billing-shape-contract)
---

### Billing Shape Contract

Billing shape contract.

#### Metadata
  * type: semantic-contract

#### Relations
  * constrain: [Billing Platform Requirement](#billing-platform-requirement)
  * use: [Billing Ontology](#billing-ontology)

#### Shapes
```turtle
@prefix billing: <https://example.test/billing#> .
@prefix ext: <https://example.test/external#> .
@prefix platform: <https://example.test/platform#> .
@prefix sh: <http://www.w3.org/ns/shacl#> .
@prefix xsd: <http://www.w3.org/2001/XMLSchema#> .

billing:InvoiceEventShape
  a sh:NodeShape ;
  sh:targetClass billing:InvoiceEvent ;
  sh:targetSubjectsOf platform:eventSubject ;
  sh:targetObjectsOf ext:externalEvent ;
  sh:property [
    sh:path (
      platform:tenant
      billing:invoice
      [ sh:zeroOrOnePath billing:lineItem ]
    ) ;
    sh:minCount 1 ;
    sh:datatype xsd:string ;
  ] .
```
