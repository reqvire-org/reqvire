### Billing Shape Contract

Billing shape contract.

#### Metadata
  * type: semantic-contract

#### Relations
  * constrain: [Billing Requirement](#billing-requirement)
  * use: [Customer Ontology](#customer-ontology)

#### Shapes
```turtle
@prefix test: <https://example.test/ontology#> .
@prefix test: <https://example.test/ontology#> .
@prefix sh: <http://www.w3.org/ns/shacl#> .

test:BillingPayloadShape
  a sh:NodeShape ;
  sh:targetClass test:BillingPayload ;
  sh:property [
    sh:path test:customerId ;
    sh:minCount 1 ;
  ] .
```
