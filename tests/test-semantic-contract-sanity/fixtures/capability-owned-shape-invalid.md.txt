### Capability Shape Contract

Capability-owned semantic contracts are invalid.

#### Metadata
  * type: semantic-contract

#### Relations
  * refine: [Access Capability](#access-capability)

#### Shapes
```turtle
@prefix access: <urn:reqvire:test:access:> .
@prefix sh: <http://www.w3.org/ns/shacl#> .
@prefix xsd: <http://www.w3.org/2001/XMLSchema#> .

access:AccessTokenShape
  a sh:NodeShape ;
  sh:targetClass access:AccessToken ;
  sh:property [
    sh:path access:expiresAt ;
    sh:minCount 1 ;
    sh:datatype xsd:string ;
  ] .
```
