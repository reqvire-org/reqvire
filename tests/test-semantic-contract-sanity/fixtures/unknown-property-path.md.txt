### Billing Shape Contract

Billing shape contract.

#### Metadata
  * type: semantic-contract

#### Relations
  * refine: [Billing Requirement](#billing-requirement)

#### Shapes
```turtle
@prefix billing: <urn:reqvire:test:billing:> .
@prefix sh: <http://www.w3.org/ns/shacl#> .

billing:CorrectiveInvoiceShape
  a sh:NodeShape ;
  sh:targetClass billing:CorrectiveInvoice ;
  sh:property [
    sh:path billing:unknownField ;
    sh:minCount 1 ;
  ] .
```
