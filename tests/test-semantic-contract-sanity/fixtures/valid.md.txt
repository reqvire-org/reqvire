### Billing Ontology

Billing ontology.

#### Metadata
  * type: ontology
  * ontology_base: https://example.test/ontology
  * ontology_prefix: test

#### Ontology
```turtle
@prefix test: <https://example.test/ontology#> .
@prefix owl: <http://www.w3.org/2002/07/owl#> .

<https://example.test/ontology> a owl:Ontology .
test:CorrectiveInvoice a owl:Class .
test:CorrectedInvoiceRef a owl:Class .
test:correctedInvoiceRef a owl:ObjectProperty .
test:status a owl:DatatypeProperty .
```

### Billing Requirement

The system shall validate billing payloads against the billing shape contract.

#### Metadata
  * type: requirement

#### Relations
  * specify: [Billing Capability](#billing-capability)
  * constrainedBy: [Billing Shape Contract](#billing-shape-contract)
---

### Billing Shape Contract

Billing shape contract.

#### Metadata
  * type: semantic-contract

#### Relations
  * constrain: [Billing Requirement](#billing-requirement)
  * use: [Billing Ontology](#billing-ontology)

#### Shapes
```turtle
@prefix test: <https://example.test/ontology#> .
@prefix rdfs: <http://www.w3.org/2000/01/rdf-schema#> .
@prefix sh: <http://www.w3.org/ns/shacl#> .
@prefix xsd: <http://www.w3.org/2001/XMLSchema#> .

test:CorrectiveInvoiceShape
  a sh:NodeShape ;
  sh:targetClass test:CorrectiveInvoice ;
  sh:property [
    sh:path rdfs:label ;
    sh:minCount 1 ;
    sh:maxCount 1 ;
    sh:datatype xsd:string ;
  ] ;
  sh:property [
    sh:path rdfs:comment ;
    sh:datatype xsd:string ;
  ] ;
  sh:property [
    sh:path test:correctedInvoiceRef ;
    sh:minCount 1 ;
    sh:maxCount 1 ;
    sh:class test:CorrectedInvoiceRef ;
  ] ;
  sh:property [
    sh:path test:status ;
    sh:datatype xsd:string ;
    sh:in ("draft" "issued" "corrected") ;
  ] .
```
