# Elements

### Cache Ontology

Cache dependency freshness.

#### Metadata
  * type: ontology
  * ontology_base: https://example.test/cache
  * ontology_prefix: local

#### External Ontology
  * prefix: ext
  * namespace: https://example.test/external#
  * resource: https://example.test/external
  * source: external.ttl
  * format: turtle

#### Ontology
```turtle
@prefix local: <https://example.test/cache#> .
@prefix ext: <https://example.test/external#> .
@prefix owl: <http://www.w3.org/2002/07/owl#> .
@prefix rdfs: <http://www.w3.org/2000/01/rdf-schema#> .
<https://example.test/cache> a owl:Ontology .
local:Resource a owl:Class ; rdfs:subClassOf ext:ExternalResource .
```
